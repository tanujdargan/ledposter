Want a fixed address? Reserve it in your router

A DHCP reservation keeps the wall's address the same on every boot without the risks of a static IP. In most routers:

  1. Open the router's admin page (often 192.168.0.1 or 192.168.1.1, printed on the router).
  2. Find DHCP settings: "Address reservation", "Static lease", "DHCP binding" or "Reserved IP".
  3. Add an entry for the wall's MAC address – with the address you want, inside the router's range.
  4. Restart the wall, or wait for its lease to renew. ledposter.local keeps working either way.
